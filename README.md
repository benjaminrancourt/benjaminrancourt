## Petit Duc

[**Petit Duc**](https://www.petitduc.ca/) is a technology platform developed by Merisia to help **events, tourism organizations and destinations** better understand and improve the mobility of their participants and visitors.

The platform combines **mobility data collection, greenhouse gas emissions measurement, participant engagement and decision-support tools** to make mobility data easier to collect, understand and act upon.

Petit Duc helps organizations measure travel-related emissions, better understand mobility patterns and move toward **more sustainable and accessible transportation**, while improving the visitor experience and supporting mobility planning.
